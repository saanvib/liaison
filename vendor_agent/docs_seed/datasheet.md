# AuroraPrep RUO Nucleic Acid Extraction Kit — Datasheet

**Catalog number:** AP-2200-RUO
**Manufacturer:** Lumen Biosciences, Inc. (fictional company, for development/testing only)
**Intended use:** For Research Use Only (RUO). Not for use in diagnostic procedures. Not intended for clinical decision-making of any kind.

## Overview

The AuroraPrep RUO Kit purifies total nucleic acid (DNA and RNA together, or DNA-only with the optional RNase step) from a range of research sample types, including cultured cells, fresh or frozen tissue, and research-grade plasma or serum. The kit uses a silica membrane spin-column chemistry with a guanidinium-based lysis buffer, avoiding the need for phenol-chloroform extraction.

## Kit contents (50-prep size, AP-2200-50)

- Lysis Buffer AL (60 mL)
- Binding Buffer AB (50 mL)
- Wash Buffer AW1 (40 mL, concentrate)
- Wash Buffer AW2 (30 mL, concentrate)
- Elution Buffer AE (15 mL)
- Proteinase K, lyophilized (2 x 10 mg)
- AuroraPrep Spin Columns (50)
- Collection Tubes, 2 mL (150)

A 200-prep size (AP-2200-200) and a 10-prep starter size (AP-2200-10) are also available.

## Performance characteristics

- **Sample input:** 10-200 mg tissue, up to 5 x 10^6 cultured cells, or 200 µL plasma/serum.
- **Typical yield:** 2-8 µg total nucleic acid from 25 mg mouse liver tissue; 0.5-2 µg from 200 µL research plasma. Actual yield depends heavily on sample type, freshness, and cell count.
- **Purity:** A260/A280 ratio typically 1.8-2.0 for DNA-enriched preps.
- **Fragment size:** Genomic DNA is predominantly double-stranded and typically 20-50 kb; the kit is not designed to recover degraded or highly fragmented DNA with high efficiency.
- **Elution volume:** 50-200 µL, user-selectable.
- **Processing time:** Approximately 35 minutes for 50 samples using the standard protocol (excludes overnight lysis option).

## Storage and stability

Store all kit components at 15-25°C (room temperature). Proteinase K should be reconstituted per the IFU and then stored at -20°C, stable for 6 months after reconstitution. Unopened kits are stable for 18 months from the date of manufacture; see the lot-specific expiration date on the box label. Do not freeze the liquid buffers.

## Compatibility

Purified nucleic acid from AuroraPrep is compatible with standard downstream molecular biology workflows, including PCR, qPCR, and most commercial library preparation kits for next-generation sequencing. Lumen Biosciences has not internally validated every third-party downstream kit; researchers should confirm compatibility with their own specific downstream application.

## Safety

Lysis Buffer AL and Wash Buffer AW1 contain guanidinium salts. Avoid contact with bleach-containing disinfectants, which can produce toxic gas. Consult the Safety Data Sheet (SDS), available on request, before use. This product is for laboratory research use by trained personnel wearing appropriate personal protective equipment.
