# AuroraPrep RUO Nucleic Acid Extraction Kit — Instructions for Use (IFU)

**For Research Use Only (RUO). Not for use in diagnostic procedures.**

This document describes the standard protocol for the AuroraPrep RUO Kit (catalog AP-2200-RUO) using fresh or frozen tissue or cultured cells as starting material. Read this entire document before beginning. Wear appropriate personal protective equipment throughout.

## Before you start

- Equilibrate all buffers to room temperature (15-25°C) if they have been refrigerated.
- Reconstitute lyophilized Proteinase K with 1.2 mL of the supplied Elution Buffer AE per vial (final concentration approximately 18 mg/mL). Reconstituted Proteinase K is stable at -20°C for up to 6 months; avoid repeated freeze-thaw cycles.
- Pre-heat a dry bath or water bath to 56°C for the lysis step.
- Label collection tubes and spin columns before starting, not after.

## Protocol: tissue or cultured cells (standard lysis)

1. Weigh 10-200 mg of tissue (or pellet up to 5 x 10^6 cultured cells) into a 2 mL tube.
2. Add 180 µL of Lysis Buffer AL and 20 µL of reconstituted Proteinase K. Mix by vortexing for 10 seconds.
3. Incubate at 56°C for 10 minutes (tissue) or until the sample is fully lysed. Tougher tissue types may require up to 30 minutes; do not exceed 60 minutes, as extended incubation can reduce DNA fragment size.
4. Add 200 µL of Binding Buffer AB. Vortex for 15 seconds. The solution should appear homogeneous; if a precipitate forms, incubate at 56°C for 3 more minutes.
5. Add 200 µL of 100% ethanol (not supplied) to the lysate. Vortex for 15 seconds.
6. Apply the entire lysate to an AuroraPrep Spin Column seated in a collection tube. Centrifuge at 8,000 x g for 1 minute. Discard the flow-through and reuse the collection tube.
7. Apply 500 µL of Wash Buffer AW1 (diluted per the label with the stated volume of 100% ethanol before first use). Centrifuge at 8,000 x g for 1 minute. Discard flow-through.
8. Apply 500 µL of Wash Buffer AW2 (diluted per the label with ethanol before first use). Centrifuge at 14,000 x g for 3 minutes to dry the membrane. Discard the collection tube.
9. Place the spin column in a clean 1.5 mL tube. Add 50-200 µL of Elution Buffer AE directly onto the membrane. Incubate at room temperature for 2 minutes.
10. Centrifuge at 14,000 x g for 1 minute. The flow-through is your purified nucleic acid. Discard the spin column.

## Optional: RNase treatment for DNA-only prep

To remove co-purified RNA, add 4 µL of RNase A (not supplied, user-sourced) to the lysate after step 3 and incubate for 2 minutes at room temperature before proceeding to step 4.

## Optional: overnight lysis

For difficult tissue (e.g., skin, cartilage), lysis may be extended by incubating overnight (12-16 hours) at 56°C instead of the standard 10-30 minutes in step 3. This document does not provide a validated protocol for lysis times beyond 16 hours.

## Storing purified nucleic acid

Store eluted nucleic acid at 4°C for short-term use (up to 1 week) or at -20°C for longer-term storage. This document does not characterize stability beyond standard freeze-thaw handling; researchers requiring long-term archival should refer to general nucleic acid storage best practices.

## Troubleshooting

- **Low yield:** Confirm input amount is within the stated range; increase incubation time at step 3 for tough samples; confirm ethanol was added at steps 5, 7, and 8.
- **Column clogging:** Reduce input amount; ensure tissue is fully homogenized before step 1.
- **Low A260/A280 purity:** Repeat the AW2 wash and dry spin (step 8) to remove residual ethanol carryover.

## Safety

Lysis Buffer AL and Wash Buffer AW1 contain guanidinium thiocyanate. Do not mix with bleach. Refer to the Safety Data Sheet before use. This kit is intended for laboratory research use by trained personnel only, and is not intended for use on or with human patient samples in any clinical context.
