# AuroraPrep RUO Kit — Internal Validation Report (Summary)

**Document type:** Validation report summary. **For Research Use Only (RUO).** This summary is provided for research reference only and is not a regulatory submission; the kit has not been evaluated by any regulatory body for diagnostic or clinical performance.

**Kit:** AuroraPrep RUO Nucleic Acid Extraction Kit, catalog AP-2200-RUO
**Study type:** Internal performance characterization across three production lots
**Sample types tested:** Mouse liver tissue, cultured HEK293 cells, research-grade pooled human plasma (de-identified, purchased research reference material; not patient samples)

## Objective

To characterize yield, purity, reproducibility, and lot-to-lot consistency of the AuroraPrep RUO Kit under the standard protocol described in the Instructions for Use.

## Methods (summary)

Three independent lots of the kit (Lots A, B, and C) were each used to process 12 replicate samples per sample type (36 replicates per sample type total), following the standard lysis protocol with no modifications. Yield was measured by UV absorbance (A260) and confirmed by fluorometric quantification on a subset of samples. Purity was assessed by A260/A280 ratio. Downstream functionality was assessed by qPCR amplification of a single-copy reference gene.

## Results

### Yield and purity

| Sample type | Mean yield | Mean A260/A280 | CV (yield) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mouse liver (25 mg) | 5.1 µg | 1.87 | 11% |
| HEK293 cells (5x10^6) | 9.8 µg | 1.91 | 9% |
| Research plasma (200 µL) | 1.1 µg | 1.79 | 18% |

Plasma showed higher variability (CV 18%) than tissue or cell samples, consistent with known donor-to-donor and freeze-thaw-history variability in cell-free nucleic acid content; this is a property of the sample type, not specifically characterized further in this study.

### Lot-to-lot consistency

Mean yield across Lots A, B, and C differed by less than 8% for all three sample types, within the acceptance range for this internal study. No lot showed a statistically significant deviation (one-way ANOVA, p > 0.05 for all sample types).

### Downstream qPCR performance

All 108 tissue and cell extracts amplified successfully with a Ct standard deviation of less than 0.4 cycles across replicates within a lot. Four of 36 plasma extracts (11%) failed to amplify within 40 cycles, consistent with the lower and more variable yield observed for that sample type.

## Limitations

This study did not include degraded or highly fragmented input samples, FFPE tissue, whole blood, saliva, or any sample type collected directly from a clinical source. This study did not compare performance against any competing commercial kit. No diagnostic sensitivity or specificity claims are made or supported by this data, because the kit is not intended, designed, or validated for diagnostic use of any kind.

## Conclusion

Under the conditions tested, the AuroraPrep RUO Kit produced consistent yield and purity across three lots for tissue, cultured cell, and research plasma samples, with downstream qPCR compatibility confirmed for tissue and cell extracts. These results support the kit's intended Research Use Only application. This report does not support, and should not be cited in support of, any clinical, diagnostic, or off-label use.
